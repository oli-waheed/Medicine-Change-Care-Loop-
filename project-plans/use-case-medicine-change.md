# Use Case: Medicine Change Review

## Actors

- Patient
- Healthcare professional

## Scenario

1. The system receives updated medicine information.
2. The system detects a medicine information change.
3. The system matches the change with a synthetic medication record.
4. A review case is created.
5. The patient can see that a relevant medicine information change has been detected.
6. The patient can request professional help.
7. A healthcare professional reviews the case.
8. The professional records a response and follow-up information.
9. The case can be closed.

## Expected Result

A medicine information change can be connected to a synthetic medication record and handled through a review workflow.

## Important Limitation

The system does not make treatment or clinical decisions.

It only detects and communicates medicine information changes.
